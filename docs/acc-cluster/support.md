# Support

Email [rccgsupport@hbku.edu.qa](mailto:rccgsupport@hbku.edu.qa).

Before you write, check [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for your error message.

To help us answer quickly, include:

- the job ID (from `Submitted batch job <jobid>`),
- the exact error message,
- the path of your job script.
